PkMultiplayer

Two-player online co-op for Gen2Recomped. Play through Pokémon Gold, Silver, Crystal — and Red, Blue, Yellow — with a friend, in the same world.

Download the latest release Source on GitHub

Walking together, emotes, chat and a 2-vs-2 double battle.

What it is

Two players, one Johto. You see each other walk, chat and send emotes, live the story's events together, trade through the Cable Club and fight trainers as a 2-vs-2 double battle, with HeartGold/SoulSilver's doubles rules.

Not an MMO: it's your own playthrough, with a friend in it. There's no server to sign up for — one of you hosts the session from inside the game, and the other joins over the local network or the internet (with ZeroTier).

It's a mod for the official Gen2Recomped: nothing to fork, just a zip you import in the launcher.

What makes it different

📖 The story, together

Events, items in the world and badges happen in both saves at once. Big battles ask your partner first.

⚔️ 2-vs-2 doubles

Against the game's own trainers and gym leaders, in lockstep, with a foe AI written for doubles. You see which move your partner picked.

🔗 Soul Link & more

The two-player Nuzlocke, a linked Day-Care, the Battle Tower in multi (Crystal), Mystery Gift and a shared Pokédex.

💬 Chat & emotes

Live chat (T), emotes (Y or the number pad) and a high five. Works on phones too.

🐾 Walking Pokémon

Your lead Pokémon follows you, HeartGold/SoulSilver-style, and your partner sees it.

🌐 English & Spanish

Switch the mod's language in its options, or let it follow the game's.

How to install

  1. Install the official Gen2Recomped and import your Gold, Silver or Crystal ROM.
  2. Download PkMultiplayer-<version>.zip from the latest release.
  3. In the launcher, open the MODS tab and press Import mod .zip (or drop the zip on the window). Make sure it's enabled.
  4. Both players need the mod, on the same version. The launcher tells you when there's a new one.

How to connect

Same networkOne player presses START → CO-OP ONLINE → HOST and gets a short code. The other goes to JOIN and types the code, or the host's IP.
Over the internetBoth join the same free ZeroTier network; the host's code already carries their ZeroTier IP. The port is UDP 7777.
If it failsThe menu says why and what to do (open UDP 7777 in the firewall, use the same mod version…). A dropped link reconnects on its own, and a battle waits for your partner to come back.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. It's a free, non-commercial fan project, and the mod's own code is MIT-licensed.

Does it include the game or a ROM?

No. You need Gen2Recomped and your own cartridge dump. The mod ships no game code and no ROM data.

Which games?

Pokémon Gold, Silver and Crystal, and Red, Blue and Yellow, through Gen2Recomped.

Can more than two people play?

The co-op is for two. Up to four more players can visit the host's world to walk, chat and emote.

PkMultiplayer en español

Co-op online para dos jugadores en Gen2Recomped. Jueguen Pokémon Oro, Plata y Cristal (y Rojo, Azul, Amarillo) con un amigo, en el mismo mundo: se ven caminar, chatean y mandan emotes, viven juntos los eventos de la historia, intercambian en el Club de Cable y pelean contra los entrenadores en combates dobles 2 contra 2.

No es un MMO: es su propia partida, con un amigo dentro. No hay servidor: uno de los dos crea la sesión desde el juego y el otro se une por la red local o por internet (con ZeroTier).

Lo que lo hace distinto

  • 📖 La historia, juntos: eventos, objetos del mapa y medallas en las dos partidas a la vez.
  • ⚔️ Dobles 2 contra 2 contra los entrenadores y líderes del juego, y ves qué ataque eligió tu compañero.
  • 🔗 Soul Link (el Nuzlocke de dos), guardería enlazada, Torre de Combate en multi (Cristal).
  • 💬 Chat (T), emotes (Y o el teclado numérico) y ¡chócala!
  • 🐾 Tu Pokémon te sigue, como en HeartGold/SoulSilver.
  • 🌐 En inglés y en español.

Instalación

  1. Instalen el Gen2Recomped oficial e importen su ROM de Oro, Plata o Cristal.
  2. Descarguen PkMultiplayer-<versión>.zip de la última versión.
  3. En el launcher: pestaña MODS → Import mod .zip.
  4. Los dos jugadores necesitan el mod, en la misma versión.

Cómo conectarse

Uno pulsa START → CO-OP ONLINE → HOST y recibe un código; el otro va a JOIN y lo escribe. Por internet, los dos entran en la misma red de ZeroTier (gratis). El puerto es UDP 7777.
